  \begin{itemize}
    \small
    \item \textbf{Reconstruction Fidelity}: Mean Squared Error (MSE) of $3.32 \times 10^{-5}$ and mean cosine similarity of $0.9916$ on 2,500 validation images.

    \item \textbf{Dictionary Mapping}: Top $90\%$ positive activations yield $\sim 26$ unique concept phrases per image.
    \item \textbf{Key Takeaway}: Near-lossless visual reconstruction is achieved under strict sparsity, but mathematical fidelity does not guarantee clinical monosemanticity.
  \end{itemize}

  \note{
    \textbf{First of all}: Let's look at how our Sparse Autoencoder reconstructs visual features.\\
    \textbf{As you see in the plots}: MSE loss drops rapidly and stabilizes at $3.32 \times 10^{-5}$, with cosine similarity reaching 0.9916.\\
    \textbf{At the same time}: High sparsity is maintained, activating only 44 out of 1024 units per image (~4.3\%).\\
    \textbf{However}: High mathematical fidelity does not automatically mean features are clinically monosemantic.
  }
\end{frame}

% --- Frame 6: Conclusions & Future Directions ---
\begin{frame}{Conclusions}{Summary \& Future Work}
  \textbf{Summary of Key Findings:}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \small
    \item \textbf{Mathematical vs Semantic Quality}: SAE achieves near-lossless visual reconstruction ($\text{MSE} = 3.32 \times 10^{-5}, L_0 \sim 4.3\%$), but text validation is bounded by reporting omission bias.
    \item \textbf{Localized Interpretability}: Alignment is domain-specific; focal anatomical clusters reach up to \textbf{49.2\% Aligned}.
    \item \textbf{Evaluator Reliability}: Frontier LLMs significantly outperform MedGemma (76\% vs 56\% human agreement).
  \end{enumerate}

  \vspace{0.2cm}
  \textbf{Future Directions:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \small
    \item Refine concept dictionaries and validate feature assignments with clinical experts.
    \item Expand human audit datasets and incorporate spatial image grounding (heatmaps/bounding boxes).
  \end{itemize}

  \note{
    \textbf{To summarize our main conclusions}:\\
    \textbf{First}: The SAE reconstructs visual features near-losslessly, but report validation is limited by omission bias.\\
    \textbf{Second}: Feature interpretability is strongly localized: specific anatomical concepts align very well with clinical text.\\
    \textbf{Third}: Automated evaluation requires strong LLMs, where frontier models clearly outperform smaller domain judges.\\
    \textbf{In the future}: We plan to refine the concept dictionary with clinicians and test spatial grounding directly on image regions. Thank you!
  }
\end{frame}
