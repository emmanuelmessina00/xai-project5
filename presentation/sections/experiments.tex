  \note{
    \textbf{Let's look} at the training results of our Sparse Autoencoder.\\
    \textbf{As you can see from the plots}: The model converges smoothly on the validation set, achieving near-lossless reconstruction with an Mean Squared Error of $3.32 \times 10^{-5}$ (three point three two times ten to the minus five) and a 0.99 cosine similarity.\\
    \textbf{This fidelity} is obtained under a strict sparsity regime. It activates only 44 out of 1024 units per image, meaning just 4.3 percent of the network is used.\\
    \textbf{Through these active neurons}: We map the visual features to our dictionary, extracting about 26 unique clinical concepts per X-ray.\\
    \textbf{However, is important that}: Mathematical fidelity does not guarantee clinical monosemanticity. A perfectly reconstructed feature might still mix different medical concepts.
  }

% --- Frame 6: Conclusions & Future Directions ---
\begin{frame}{Conclusions}{Summary \& Future Work}
  \textbf{Summary of Key Findings:}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \small
    \item \textbf{Text-to-Image Mismatch}: The SAE is highly accurate mathematically ($L_0 \sim 4.3\%$), but report-based validation is severely bottlenecked by clinical omission bias.
    \item \textbf{Localized Interpretability}: Clinical alignment is not uniform; specific semantic clusters perform better (up to \textbf{49.2\% Aligned}).
    \item \textbf{Evaluator Reliability}: Frontier LLMs significantly outperform MedGemma (76\% vs 56\% human agreement).
  \end{enumerate}

  \vspace{0.2cm}
  \textbf{Future Directions:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \small
    \item Refine concept dictionaries and validate semantic assignments with clinical experts.
    \item Move beyond pure text evaluation by incorporating spatial grounding (heatmaps/bounding boxes).
  \end{itemize}

  \note{
    \textbf{To summarize our key findings}:\\
    \textbf{First}: The SAE reconstructs visual features near-losslessly, but report validation is limited by omission bias.\\
    \textbf{Second}: The interpretability is localized, with certain semantic sub-domains aligning better.\\
    \textbf{Third}: Automated evaluation requires strong LLMs, where frontier models clearly outperform smaller domain judges.\\
    \textbf{For future work}: We suggest moving beyond text-only validation. Grounding concepts directly onto the image space via heatmaps, combined with clinical expert validation, is the necessary next step. Thank you.
  }
\end{frame}
