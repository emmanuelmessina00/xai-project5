\begin{frame}{Introduction \& Abstract}{Unsupervised Concept Discovery in VLMs}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Objective}: Investigate unsupervised concept discovery in medical Vision-Language Models (VLMs) without diagnostic supervision[cite: 3].
    \item \textbf{Methodology}: A Sparse Autoencoder (SAE) decomposes frozen BiomedCLIP embeddings into $1,024$ latent features[cite: 3].
    \item \textbf{Vocabulary Generation}: Features are named using 998 phrases associated with 100 clinical concepts, extracted without relying on downstream disease labels[cite: 3].
    \item \textbf{Semantic Evaluation}: Extracted concepts are assessed against Open-I radiology reports using a frozen MedGemma evaluator[cite: 3].
    \item \textbf{Key Findings}:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item SAE achieves near-lossless reconstruction with high sparsity, but textual alignment remains low on average ($7.21\%$ Aligned)[cite: 3].
        \item Alignment is highly uneven across clinical subdomains[cite: 3].
        \item Evaluator reliability is a critical bottleneck: MedGemma agreed with human consensus only $56\%$ of the time[cite: 3].
    \end{itemize}
  \end{itemize}
