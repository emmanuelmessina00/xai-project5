\section{Research gaps}
Three gaps guide our analysis. First, an SAE's sparsity and reconstruction fidelity do not guarantee that a nearest text label correctly describes its feature: vocabulary coverage and the image--text geometry constrain naming. Second, a report is selective evidence. An unmentioned concept may be visible, absent, or incorrectly named, and an external judge can additionally confuse omission with contradiction. Third, independently assigned labels omit relationships between concepts. We examine whether semantic clustering produces useful groups, while recognizing that embedding similarity does not establish an anatomical hierarchy or causal relation. Our human audit addresses evaluator reliability; it does not establish image-level clinical correctness.
