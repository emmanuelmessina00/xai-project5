\section{Conclusions}
We implemented a MedConcept-inspired pipeline that discovers sparse features in chest X-ray embeddings, assigns clinical phrases, and evaluates their report agreement. Reconstruction is strong on the inspected validation batch, but Open-I alignment is low and uncertainty is high. Clustering reveals uneven agreement across concept groups, while the human audit shows that evaluator errors materially limit interpretation of the scores. The main outcome is therefore a working, inspectable pipeline and a critical assessment of its evidence. Future work should prioritize expert verification, controlled baselines, vocabulary independence, and stability testing before making claims of clinically faithful concept discovery.
